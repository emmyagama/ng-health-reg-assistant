# Sanitisation Protocol

This protocol governs everything published from this project and any 
future client-facing work.

- No client names.
- No patient data.
- Band sensitive numbers (use ranges, not exact figures, where numbers 
  could identify a specific client or situation).
- Describe roles, never people.
- Firm clearance required before every publication.
- This project's builds stay unmixed with any client data.
- Never publish the firm's named instruments (templates, proprietary 
  tools, or internal document formats).
