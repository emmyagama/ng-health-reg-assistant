A diagnostic centre manager in Nigeria cannot quickly find what the regulations require of their facility. The rules are spread across several long PDFs from different bodies, and searching them by hand takes hours and misses things. B1 is a small assistant that answers plain-language questions from a fixed set of public documents: the NDPA and NDPC guidance, the NHIA Act, HEFAMAA facility requirements, MLSCN guidelines, the National Health Act 2014 and WHO laboratory-quality guidance. It names the document and section behind every answer. It refuses rather than guesses when the documents don’t cover the question, and it refuses clinical questions altogether. Its reliability is measured on a 30-question test set that includes 5 questions the documents cannot answer, run before and after guardrails.
